# NNNN. Short title in the imperative

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by [NNNN](NNNN-....md)
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What forces are at play? What makes this decision non-obvious? State the
problem, not the solution. If there is evidence - an incident, a measurement, a
paper - cite it here rather than asserting.

## Decision

What was decided, in the active voice: "We do X." One decision per record.

## Consequences

What follows from this, good and bad. Name the costs explicitly - an ADR that
lists only benefits is marketing, not a decision record. Include what a future
contributor would otherwise be tempted to "fix" without knowing the history.
